About EduPath AI

A learning companion designed to help students turn career ambitions into practical, trackable learning journeys.

🎯

The Problem

Students can struggle to choose what to learn, which resources to use and what to do next.

🧭

Our Solution

EduPath AI organizes learning into career-focused roadmaps, milestones and resource links.

📊

Our Goal

Make learning progress easier to understand, track and improve over time.

Built for student learning

This hackathon prototype demonstrates roadmap generation, resource discovery, progress tracking and a browser-based admin dashboard.

The current version uses local browser storage. A production deployment needs a backend, a database and server-side authorization.
